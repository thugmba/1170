LANGUAGE BRIDGE

Four terms you will use today

Signal

A recorded customer action that may help answer a business question: a save, a completed view, a search, or a repeat visit.

Recommendation

A suggested next product, item, or piece of content selected for a customer.

Feedback loop

A repeated process in which an observed result improves the next decision.

Churn

The percentage of paying customers who cancel in a period. It helps show whether people stay.

CORE CONCEPT 01

Data is not automatically a business asset

A data point becomes useful only when it helps answer a business question and changes an action.

More data without a better decision is storage, not advantage.

Do not confuse a click with a need

A high number of clicks may mean interest, confusion, or a failed search. Start with the decision you want to improve, then choose the smallest useful signal.

MICRO-APPLICATION / COMPLETE THE LOOPSLIDE 8 / 15
CORE CONCEPT 03

Quality, context, and trust set the limit

Relevant

Does this signal help the specific decision we need to make?

Contextual

Could another explanation produce the same customer action?

Trusted

Do customers understand and accept how the data is collected and used?

Privacy and trust are business conditions. Without them, customers may leave or provide less reliable information.

MICRO-APPLICATION / CONTEXT CHECKSLIDE 10 / 15
CORE CONCEPT 04

Customer economics show whether the loop creates value

CACCustomer Acquisition Cost: sales and marketing cost needed to acquire one paying customer.
LTVCustomer Lifetime Value: gross profit expected from a customer before that customer leaves.
ChurnThe percentage of paying customers who cancel in a period.
ARPUAverage Revenue Per User or Member: revenue earned from each active customer or member.
These measures do not prove that a recommendation is good. They show whether a better experience is also improving the business.
